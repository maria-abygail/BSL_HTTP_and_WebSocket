About me

Network-minded, team-minded and happiest near the water.

I’m a student at MSU-IIT with a growing interest in how networks work and how to protect them. I’ve studied LAN fundamentals, Ethernet, switching and MAC addressing, along with network security basics, access control and graph-based security concepts.

Beyond the classroom, I’ve served in student government, led a publication team and mentored students through retreats. Those roles taught me how to plan, document and communicate clearly. I also work as a photographer assistant, which keeps my eye for detail sharp.

I’m always open to learning opportunities, collaborations and conversations with people in technology.
